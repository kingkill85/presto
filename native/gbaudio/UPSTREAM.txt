Experimental separate gbaudiocore module reuses ../gb/gbcore.c and its
Peanut-GB license. Uses all four APU channels, s16 stereo at nominal 22050 Hz,
mixed to mono PWM words. 369 samples/frame are played at 22039 Hz to match
the DMG frame period, avoiding cumulative buffer drift. Audio register
writes are frame-granular: this is not cycle-accurate original hardware audio.
Initialisation powers NR52 before applying the upstream boot defaults.
No audio thread, IRQ handler or retained stack pointers. PCM scratch is
stack-local; only the caller-owned output buffer is passed to DMA.
Production integration: exports versioned presto-gb-audio-v2 full states
including the pointer-free APU context; old CPU-only snapshots are rejected.
The normal DMG launcher now selects this module. retro_audio.py supplies
finite forward-linked DMA buffers; the silent gbcore remains for diagnostics.
